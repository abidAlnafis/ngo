Our Story

Born from the Heart of Bangladesh

Disa Bangladesh was established in 2005 by a group of passionate Bangladeshi social workers who believed that lasting change is possible only when it comes from within the community itself. Starting with a single school in Manikganj, we grew organically — driven not by grants alone, but by the unwavering trust of the communities we serve.

Today, we operate across 23 districts, running programs in education, healthcare, women empowerment, environmental conservation, and economic development. Our approach is always grassroots: we listen first, then act.

Disa (আশা) means "Hope" in Bengali — and hope is exactly what we bring to every village, every family, and every child we touch.

Our Mission

Mission, Vision & Values

🎯

Our Mission

To empower marginalized communities in Bangladesh through education, healthcare, and sustainable livelihood programs that build resilience and restore dignity.

🔭

Our Vision

A Bangladesh where every person — regardless of gender, geography, or circumstance — has equal access to opportunity, health, and a life of purpose.

💎

Our Promise

We promise every donor, volunteer, and community member that we will be transparent, accountable, and relentlessly focused on creating measurable positive change.

Our Journey

Two Decades of Impact

2005

Foundation Established

Disa Bangladesh registered in Dhaka with 5 founding members and one primary school in Manikganj serving 120 children.

2008

Healthcare Program Launch

Launched the first mobile health unit, providing free medical services to 3 rural upazilas in Barisal division.

2011

Women Empowerment Initiative

Started vocational training for women. First cohort of 200 women completed sewing and handicraft courses in Rajshahi.

2015

National Scale-Up

Expanded to 15 districts with support from BRAC and international donors. Reached our first milestone of 10,000 direct beneficiaries.

2019

Climate Resilience Program

Launched environmental and climate adaptation programs for flood-prone communities in Sylhet and Khulna divisions.

2025

20 Years of Hope

Celebrating 20 years with operations in 23 districts, 50,000+ lives touched, and a commitment to serving the next generation of Bangladeshis.
