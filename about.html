Our Background

Development for Ideal Social Association

Development for Ideal Social Association in Bangladesh (DISA) is a non-profit, non-governmental, and non-political organization dedicated to uplifting underprivileged communities by securing their basic rights and improving socio-economic conditions.

Founded in 1997, DISA operates across 17 Upazilas in the Jamalpur region, focusing on marginalized groups — mostly poor, illiterate, and landless farmers and laborers. Our dedicated team works tirelessly to empower these communities through sustainable development projects, skill training, and social awareness.

Disa (দিশা) means "Hope" in Bengali — and hope is exactly what we bring to every village, every family, and every child we touch.

Our Journey

Two Decades of Impact

1997

Foundation Established

DISA was founded in Jamalpur, starting its journey to uplift marginalized communities through sustainable development.

2008

Healthcare Program Launch

Launched the first mobile health unit, providing free medical services to rural upazilas in Jamalpur and beyond.

2011

Women Empowerment Initiative

Started vocational training for women. First cohort of 200 women completed sewing and handicraft courses.

2015

National Scale-Up

Expanded operations with support from international donors. Reached our first milestone of 10,000 direct beneficiaries.

2019

Climate Resilience Program

Launched environmental and climate adaptation programs for flood-prone communities in climate-vulnerable areas.

2025

Moving Forward

Working in 17 Upazilas, touching thousands of lives through community-driven sustainable change.
