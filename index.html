Our Story

Rooted in Bangladesh,
Driven by Compassion

Founded in 2005 in Dhaka, Disa Bangladesh started with a simple belief: every child deserves education, every family deserves healthcare, and every woman deserves dignity. Our grassroots approach ensures that aid reaches those who need it most.

Over two decades, we have expanded from a single district to 23 districts across Bangladesh, touching over 50,000 lives directly. We work hand in hand with local communities, government bodies, and international partners to drive sustainable, long-term change.

Our Principles

What Guides Us

🤝

Community First

Every decision we make centers on the needs, voices, and dignity of the communities we serve across Bangladesh.

💚

Transparency

We maintain complete financial transparency. Every taka donated is accounted for and reported publicly to our stakeholders.

🌱

Sustainability

We design programs that empower communities to sustain progress independently, long after our projects conclude.

⚖️

Equity & Inclusion

We prioritize the most marginalized, ensuring women, children, and minorities receive equitable access to our services.

🔬

Evidence-Based

Our programs are designed and continuously improved based on rigorous data collection, research, and measurable impact.

🌍

Partnerships

We collaborate with government agencies, UN bodies, and local organizations to maximize reach and effectiveness.
