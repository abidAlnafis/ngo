Our Approach

Driven by Data, Rooted in Reality

At DISA Bangladesh, we believe that effective social programs must be built on a foundation of rigorous research and evidence. Our dedicated research team works in the field to collect data, analyze trends, and measure the direct impact of our interventions.

We collaborate with academic institutions and development partners to publish insights that help improve policies and practices across the NGO sector in Bangladesh.

Key Focus Areas

Our Research Priorities

🎓

Education Accessibility

Studying the barriers to primary and secondary education for children in remote and climate-vulnerable regions.

🤒

Health Disparities

Analyzing health outcomes and access to maternal and child healthcare services in rural upazilas.

💰

Economic Mobility

Investigating the impact of microfinance and vocational training on long-term poverty reduction and self-reliance.
